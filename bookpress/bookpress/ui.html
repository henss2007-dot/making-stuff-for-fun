How to write your book

The text box is your whole book. Everything between the two --- lines at the top is settings; everything after is the book itself.

Structure

# The StormA chapter (numbered automatically: “Chapter One”, “Chapter Two”…). Writing # Chapter 3: The Storm also works.
#* PrologueA chapter without a number (prologue, epilogue, about the author…)
@part Part OneA part-divider page before a group of chapters
## A headingA small heading inside a chapter
* * *A scene break

Text

blank lineSeparates paragraphs. Quotes and dashes become real “curly quotes” and — dashes automatically.
*italic* **bold**Emphasis
> textA quoted / indented paragraph
| linePoetry or song: each line stays on its own line (leading spaces are kept)
-> textA centered line
two spaces at end of lineKeeps a line break inside a paragraph

Settings (top of the file)

title, subtitle, authorUsed on the cover, title page and running heads
year, publisher, publisher_city, isbn, edition, rights, fiction: trueCopyright page details
dedication, epigraph, epigraph_sourceExtra front-matter pages (use \n for a line break)
cover_image: images/cover.jpgYour own cover picture, filling the page. Add cover_text: true to print the title over it. Without a cover image you get a typographic cover (cover_color, cover_accent).
cover: falseNo cover page at all (for print-on-demand interiors)
trim: 6x9Page size: 5x8, 5.5x8.5, 6x9, 7x10, a5, a4, letter, pocket, or e.g. 14x21cm
font: GeorgiaAny serif font installed on your computer (Palatino Linotype, Georgia, Garamond, Book Antiqua, Times New Roman, EB Garamond…), or a path to a .ttf file / folder of .ttf files
font_size: 11, leading: 15.4Type size and line spacing in points
toc: false, drop_caps: false, blank_pages: false, the_end: true, chapter_label: Kapitel, scene_break: ~Switch the table of contents, drop caps, blank left-hand pages (chapters always start on the right like a printed book), a THE END line, the chapter word, the scene-break ornament

Lines starting with // are notes to yourself and are not printed.
