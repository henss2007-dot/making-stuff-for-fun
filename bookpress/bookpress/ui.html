Settings written into the top of your file

How to write your book

The text box is your whole book. Everything between the two --- lines at the top is settings (the Settings button edits them for you); everything after is the book itself.

Structure

# The StormA chapter, numbered automatically (“Chapter One”…). # Chapter 3: The Storm also works.
#* PrologueA chapter without a number (prologue, epilogue, about the author…)
@part Part OneA part-divider page
@subtitle … / @epigraph Quote -- WhoA subtitle or an epigraph under a chapter title (put them right after the # line)
## Heading / ### SmallerHeadings inside a chapter
* * *A scene break
@pagebreakStart a new page. @blank starts on a right-hand page.

Text

blank lineSeparates paragraphs. Quotes and dashes become the proper marks for your language.
*italic* **bold** ***both***Emphasis. Also __underline__, ~~strike~~, ^^small caps^^, x^2^, H,,2,,O, `typewriter`, {red}colour{/}
text[^A note]A footnote (printed at the end of the chapter, or in a Notes chapter)
> textA quoted / indented paragraph
| linePoetry or song: each line stays on its own line, leading spaces kept
-> textA centered line
- item / 1. itemBullet and numbered lists
| a | b | + |---|---|A table (first row is the header; |---:| right-aligns a column)
::: box Title … :::A framed box. Also ::: note (shaded), ::: letter (italic, indented), ::: right, ::: center
two spaces at end of lineKeeps a line break inside a paragraph
// noteA note to yourself, not printed

Pictures

![Caption](images/boat.jpg)A picture with a caption, as wide as the text. Use + Image…, or drop / paste a picture into the editor.
…{width=50%}Narrower (also width=3in, 8cm, height=2in)
…{wrap=right}Small picture with the next paragraph flowing around it
![a](x.jpg) ![b](y.jpg)Two or three pictures side by side on one line
…{page}A picture on its own page (a plate)
…{border} {align=left} {nocaption}Border, alignment, hide the caption

Looks, languages and outputs

Open Settings. Start with a preset (classic novel, modern, manuscript, poetry, non-fiction, large print, children) and a language (35 languages, including Japanese, Chinese and Korean), then adjust anything: page size, fonts, margins, paragraph style, chapter opener style, running heads, page numbers, cover design, pictures, contents, front and back matter, printing.

Output decides what you get: book (a complete PDF, like a bound book), screen (no blank pages, for phones), booklet (a second PDF with two pages per sheet for folding and stapling at home), print (an interior without the cover plus a separate wrap-around cover PDF with spine and barcode box, for Lulu, KDP, Blurb…).

Fonts: any font installed on your computer works. The Settings panel can also download free fonts (EB Garamond, Lora, Noto Serif JP…). Or run bookpress fonts in a terminal.
